Chapter 0. Introduction to the Loca Deserta Sci-Fi Universe

Welcome to Loca Deserta Sci-Fi (Дике Поле Sci-Fi) — the pioneering universe of Ukrainian military space fiction! Up until Hetman Ivan Vyhovskyi took power in 1657, history in our world and Loca Deserta Sci-Fi was identical. In this timeline, the Cossack Hetmanate not only survived but expanded across Eastern Europe and the Balkans.

By the 19th century, three superpowers governed Earth: the Empire of the Sun (Asia & the Pacific), the Corporate Republic (the Americas & Australia), and our homeland — the Hetmanate Federation (all of Europe, North Africa, the Middle East, and Asia Minor).

In 2143 AD (Year 0 of the Star Era — SE), scientists aboard the Europa Jovian International Research Station invented the warp drive powered by superheavy Lawrencium and Nobelium inside Jupiter's gravity well.

The Martian Division of the Universe (33 SE) & Colony "Wild Field-17"

Following the bloody Teegarden B Incident in 31 SE, the three empires signed the Martian Treaty on July 24, 33 SE, demilitarizing the Solar System and dividing deep space into three eternal sectors. In 91 SE, the Hetmanate Federation established Star Sich (Зоряна Січ) in the Virgo Supercluster.
