❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Loca Deserta (Wild Field) Universe?
Loca Deserta (Ukrainian: Дике Поле / Wild Field) is an independent Ukrainian video game and sci-fi book universe created by developer and author Dmytro Gladkyi (@dmytrogladkyi). It spans two eras: XVII-century Cossack Ukraine (Loca Deserta: Odesa, Sloboda 2, Chumaki, Sloboda, Interactive Stories, Hex) and the 25th-century Loca Deserta Sci-Fi universe featuring the Hetmanate Federation (books "Star Hetmanate", "Encounter in the Steppe", "Fist's Edge", and the strategy game Loca Deserta: Settlers).
Which Loca Deserta games can I play for free in my web browser?
Five games are playable directly in your browser with zero installation: Loca Deserta: Settlers (settlers.locadeserta.com), Loca Deserta: Chumaki (locadeserta.com/chumaki), Loca Deserta: Sloboda (locadeserta.com/sloboda), Loca Deserta Interactive Stories (locadeserta.com/game/?lang=en), and Loca Deserta: Hex (locadeserta.com/hex).
Where can I get Loca Deserta games on Steam, Epic Games, iOS,Android, Fortnite, and Roblox?
Loca Deserta: Odesa and Loca Deserta: Sloboda 2 are available on Steam, Epic Games Store, Apple App Store, and Google Play. In addition, 9 custom Fortnite Islands are published under creator code @dmytrogladkyi, and a 20v20 Cossacks vs. Tatars multiplayer shooter is playable on Roblox.
Where can I read the Loca Deserta Sci-Fi books?
All three novels in the Star Hetmanate series ("Zoryanij Hetmanat", "Zustrich v Stepu", and "Vistrya Kulaku") can be read online for free at locadeserta.com/locadesertascifi/, on Booknet, downloaded as EPUB from GitHub Releases, or purchased on Apple Books.
