Loca Deserta: Chumaki

Step into the boots of a XVII-century Chumak merchant in Ukraine! Start at Zaporozhian Sich with a single wagon, compare prices across 25+ historical towns on the Boplan map, hire Otamans, unlock luxury goods (silk, cannons, amber), and build town manufactories.

▶ Play Online in Browser 🤖 Google Play 🍎 App Store 💻 Windows ZIP 🍏 macOS ZIP 📦 Android APK

Core Gameplay Features

  • Trading & Global Market: Compare town stocks and profit margins across the entire map before dispatching your wagons.
  • Otamans & Perks: Hire Otaman leaders for each wagon to earn XP and unlock high-margin luxury goods.
  • Town Manufactories: Build and upgrade manufactories up to level 3 in over 25 historical Ukrainian towns.
  • Handcrafted City Events: Complete custom tasks for townsfolk to earn large gold rewards and Otaman experience.
